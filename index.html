2 · What sits behind an answer

Five kinds of source data answer five different questions.

A good question starts by choosing the right evidence—not by asking the connector to “tell us the market.”

01 · Customs / UN Comtrade
Where goods moved

Imports and exports by reporter, month, HS code and partner country.

Example: which countries supplied US nickel imports last quarter?
02 · USGS / industry bodies
Where minerals are produced

Country production, reserves and concentration estimates.

Example: how exposed is chromium supply to a few producers?
03 · Official notices
What rules may change

Export measures, government notices and dated policy evidence.

Example: has a producing country announced an export restriction?
04 · Counterparty directory
Who may be relevant

Potential miners, refiners, processors or traders, subject to plan access.

Example: which processors could be worth a sales discussion?
05 · Models & assumptions
What might happen

Scenario calculations for grade, recovery, route cost or cash cycle.

Example: how would lower recovery change a shipment margin?
Delivery layer
Reports + AI connectors

MiningBridge organises claims and sources into briefs; the connectors retrieve these, subject to scope and permissions.

Example: ask for a brief, then validate its source period.
Sources: data provenance and caveats · tool descriptions · models

6 · Our pitch

One decision chain: ore to cash to commitments.

Gulf Mining does not need a generic “mining AI.” It needs an operating record that shows whether a proposed commercial action is physically possible, profitable and fundable.

01 · MINEAvailable ore

Site, grade, assay, extractable quantity and expected cost.

02 · PLANTConversion plan

Recovery, capacity, yield, production timing and cost.

03 · CUSTOMERSales opportunity

Specification, buyer, contract terms, route, freight and receipt date.

04 · TREASURYCash and obligations

Funding requirement, forecast balance and debt dues in the same period.

Where MiningBridge fits

External research panel

Store a mineral brief, cited source, publication date, trade or supply observation, and analyst interpretation beside the opportunity.

Where our software fits

Internal decision record

Calculate the case from Gulf Mining’s data, compare alternatives, route for approval, and track whether the expected cash actually arrived.

Management output: “If we accept this order, what will be produced, what margin may it earn, when will cash arrive, and which existing obligations fall due first?”

2 · VALIDATEProduction

3 · MODELEconomics

Use actual cost and freight quotations to estimate margin and working capital.

→
4 · REVIEWCash & approval

Compare receipt timing with repayment dues; record owner and decision.

Illustrative question

Should we accept a 1,000-tonne order?

Suppose buyer payment arrives 60 days after shipment. Production, freight and debt instalments may fall earlier. The screen would show the gap and the assumptions behind it.

1,000 tonnes and 60 days are invented solely to explain the workflow.

Expected pilot output

One reviewable decision sheet

Product feasibility · expected margin · peak cash need · source evidence · key sensitivities · approval trail · later actual-versus-plan result.

No external intelligence should overwrite an approved internal financial record automatically.
